# Interview Scheduling Policy

All interview rounds must be scheduled with a minimum notice period of 48 hours communicated in writing to the candidate. Candidates are permitted to reschedule an assessment round at most once with at least 24 hours prior written notice to the talent acquisition coordinator. Each technical interview panel must consist of at least two certified technical interviewers to ensure unbiased evaluation. Post-interview feedback forms must be officially submitted within 24 hours of interview completion by all panel members.
