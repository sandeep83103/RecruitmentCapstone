# Employee Probation Period Policy

The standard probation duration for all new hires is 6 months starting from the verified date of joining. A formal 30-day performance review milestone is conducted between the employee and line manager to calibrate role expectations and identify training needs. At the culmination of the 6-month cycle, HR issues an official confirmation letter based on manager endorsement. In instances of sub-par performance, management reserves the right to extend the probation window by up to an additional 3 months.
