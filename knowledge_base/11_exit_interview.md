# Exit Interview and Separation Policy

All departing permanent employees must complete a mandatory confidential HR exit interview feedback session prior to their final working day. The session captures candid feedback regarding workplace culture, compensation equity, and leadership effectiveness. Assets including corporate laptops, security tokens, and employee ID badges must be returned before initiating the final full and final settlement. HR processes full and final settlements within 45 working days following the issuance of the official relieving letter.
