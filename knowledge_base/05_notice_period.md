# Notice Period and Buyout Policy

The standard contractual notice period for confirmed permanent staff is 90 days across all engineering and management positions. Company-sponsored notice period buyouts between 30 and 90 days are evaluated on business criticality and require Vice President level authorization. Candidates opting for notice period buyouts must secure an unconditional relieving letter and experience certificate from their immediate previous employer. Serving notice without prior relieving documentation prevents official onboarding onto the enterprise payroll system.
