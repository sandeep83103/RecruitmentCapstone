# Employee Referral Bonus Policy

The employee referral bonus payout is disbursed in two equal milestones: 50% on joining and 50% after 90 days. Referrals must be officially logged in the recruitment portal prior to the candidate's initial screening call. Direct hiring managers and executive leadership are exempt from receiving referral disbursements for vacancies within their reporting hierarchy. Employees referring qualified candidates receive standard financial referral disbursements upon successful candidate selection.
