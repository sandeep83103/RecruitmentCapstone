# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

Can I work remotely? Employees are permitted to work remotely for 2 days per week under the hybrid remote work model with operational manager approval. The organization operates under a hybrid work model comprising 2 days of remote work and 3 days of in-office presence each week. Employees working remotely must maintain core working hours availability and adhere strictly to enterprise virtual private network protocols. Fully remote exceptions may only be approved for specialized engineering units under specific regional director sanction.
