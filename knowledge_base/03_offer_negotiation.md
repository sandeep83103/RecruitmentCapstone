# Offer Negotiation Policy

Employment offers must adhere strictly to predefined compensation CTC bands established for each grade and department. Discretionary sign-on bonuses may be extended solely for critical niche skillsets subject to formal written approval from the Compensation and Benefits leadership. Candidates are granted a 5-day decision window from the timestamp of offer letter issuance to accept or decline the formal offer. Failure to accept within the 5-day window without an authorized extension will result in automated offer revocation.
